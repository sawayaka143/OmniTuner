OmniTuner is a free, installable practice workbench for guitar and other acoustic instruments. It listens through your microphone, detects pitch in real time, and pairs a chromatic tuner with three practice tools — all in a single web app that works offline after the first visit.

What's inside

  • Tuner — real-time pitch detection with a cents readout, automatic or manual string targeting, an adjustable reference pitch (A4), and in-tune sound and glow feedback.
  • Scales — an interactive fretboard for any root note and scale, with playback, custom tunings, and note or degree labels.
  • Chords — voicing search across your tuning, shown as tab, dots, or a neck diagram, plus progression building and key detection.
  • Metronome — tap tempo, meter and polyrhythm patterns, count-in, subdivisions, and per-role sounds.

How it works

OmniTuner is an Angular progressive web app. Pitch detection runs in a Web Worker over the Web Audio API, so your audio is analysed on your device and never uploaded. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking — your settings live in your browser's local storage.

Source code
