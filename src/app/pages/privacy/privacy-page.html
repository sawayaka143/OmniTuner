OmniTuner is built to keep your audio and your data on your own device. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not collect. It is published by {{ publisher }}.

Your microphone

When you start the tuner, your browser asks for permission to use your microphone. Audio is analysed in real time on your device using the Web Audio API and a Web Worker. No audio is recorded, transmitted, or stored anywhere — it never leaves your device. Analysis stops the moment you stop the tuner or leave the page.

What is stored

Your preferences are saved in your browser's localStorage so the app remembers your setup between visits. Nothing is sent to a server. The keys used are:

  • omnituner.tuner-preferences.v1 — tuner settings and custom tunings
  • omnituner.scales.v1 — scale and fretboard preferences
  • omnituner.metronome.v1 — metronome settings and presets
  • omnituner.instruments.v1 — your instrument definitions
  • omnituner.theme.v1 — light or dark theme
  • small UI keys such as window position and panel width

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. OmniTuner sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising, and contains no third-party trackers. There are no user accounts and no sign-in.

Third-party requests

The interface loads its typeface from Google Fonts and its icon set from jsDelivr. As with any web request, those providers receive your IP address and browser user agent. No other third-party services are contacted, and the app works offline once loaded.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact page.
